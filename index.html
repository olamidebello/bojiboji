One ball. Everyone runs.

Gather in the circle. The toss chooses a seeker. Hide behind cover, dodge the ball, and survive the countdown. The final survivor wins.


How to play

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the touch arrows. Aim with your mouse or by tapping the field; click the field or press Space / Throw to throw. On touch screens, moving also sets your aim. Cover blocks players and balls. Each round starts with a random toss and a 4-second hiding phase. A hit transfers the ball immediately; a miss returns it after the ball stops. The new seeker gets a brief pause so the previous seeker can escape. At zero, the current seeker is eliminated—even if their throw is in flight. Eliminated players can watch the remaining rounds.
