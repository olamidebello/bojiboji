How you go play

Use WASD, arrow keys or the arrows for screen to move. Point mouse or tap field to aim; click field, press Space or Throw to throw ball. For touch screen, direction wey you move go also set your aim. Cover go block players and ball. Each round start with random ball toss, then 4 seconds to hide. If ball hit person, na that person go collect am. If you miss, ball go return after e stop. New ball holder go pause small so the former one fit escape. When time reach zero, person wey hold ball don lose that round, even if ball still dey fly. Person wey don comot fit watch the rest.

Where you go hide

Describe where you go hide, add picture, and choose the place for game field. People for your room go see your profile. Use general place name, no put your private address.

You never choose place

Choose open place wey no get cover. Hiding profile no go add new cover or carry player jump go another place.
