# Bojiboji visual identity

Slogan: **Hide. Hunt. Hit.**

The running figures and circular arrangement represent the changing seeker role. The orange ball anchors the mark; forest green, lime and cream connect it to the village-courtyard setting.

## Project assets

- `assets/bojiboji-logo.webp`: generated logo, used in the game header.
- `assets/bojiboji-key-art.webp`: generated village-courtyard artwork, used in the landing hero.
- `assets/boji-mark.svg`: lightweight circle-and-ball favicon.
- `assets/arena-art.svg`: supplementary vector courtyard illustration retained in the project.

The logo and key art were created with the built-in image generation tool. Original generated PNGs were converted to WebP for delivery without changing their composition. Both generated outputs were visually inspected before integration.

## Final logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create an original premium BOJIBOJI game logo on a solid very dark forest green background #102a25. A clean bold compact emblem: three abstract running human figures forming a broken circular chase around a small orange rubber ball. Rounded energetic silhouettes, forward motion, friendly outdoor ball-tag game and hide-and-seek concept. Lime green, warm cream and orange. Beneath the emblem place the exact word BOJIBOJI in beautifully crafted bold rounded uppercase lettering, perfectly spelled B O J I B O J I. Under it a small tagline exactly 'HIDE. HUNT. HIT.' Flat crisp vector-like design, high legibility, minimal shapes, centered composition, no mockup, no gradients, no extra text, no watermark. Square brand asset.

## Final key-art prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a premium wide landscape game key art for BOJIBOJI, an original Nigerian-inspired circle, hide-and-seek and soft-ball tag game. Warm village courtyard at sunset, lush mango trees, low ochre walls and playful hiding spots. Five diverse young adult African friends in colorful contemporary clothes, one at center gently tossing a small orange rubber ball towards a running friend, others laughing and ducking behind trees and walls. Clear readable chase story, energetic welcoming expressions, no aggression, no combat. Subtle chalk circle on the earth refers to their starting circle. Beautiful polished stylized 3D animated game illustration, cinematic golden rim light, forest green/lime/apricot brand palette. Wide composition, no text, no watermark, no UI. Intended as website hero art.
